On Building Things

I've been thinking a lot lately about what it means to build something well. Not just code that compiles or a feature that ships — but something that feels right. Design Isn't Decoration

February 22, 2026

One of the biggest misconceptions I had early on was that design is something you add on top. You build the thing, then you make it pretty. But the more I work on real projects, the more I realize design is the structure itself. It's the decisions about what to show and what to hide, when to ask and when to assume. Good design disappears.

Learning to Lead

January 10, 2026

Leading a team of eight people through a full development cycle taught me something I didn't expect: the hardest part isn't making decisions, it's creating space for other people to make good ones. You can't just have a vision — you have to communicate it clearly enough that everyone can run with it in their own way.
