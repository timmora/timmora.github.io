Origin

I'm Tim, a recent CS + Education graduate from UIUC. I've been a builder for as long as I can remember, and code just happened to be the medium I chose to help me bring my spontaneous ideas to life!

Practice

Throughout my exploration of computer science, I grew increasingly drawn to the relationship between technology and people. This eventually brought me to UX design and frontend development, where a product's success is decided largely by how it feels to use, instead of just whether it runs. Most recently, I led an 8-person team through a full software development cycle and served as the Design Lead on HoloOrbits, an educational game from Professor Jina Kang's team at UIUC that teaches the fundamentals of space physics through collaborative play. In both roles, the process was the same: start from the person on the other side of the screen and work backwards.

Outside

Outside the screen, I have profound love for writing, music, and pretty much anything creative. I've always thought that this background makes me a better builder. I'm crazy about fine details and consistency, I care deeply about feel, and I ask questions that don't always show up in a product brief.

Next

I'm currently looking for opportunities where I can bring both sides to the table, both the technical foundation and the human instinct. If that sounds like something you're building toward, I'd love to connect!
