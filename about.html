Origin

I'm Tim, a recent CS + Education graduate from UIUC. I've been a builder for as long as I can remember, and code just happened to be the material I chose to help me bring my spontaneous ideas to life!

Practice

I've always been drawn to the relationship between technology and people. That's what eventually brought me to UX design and frontend development, where a product's success is decided by how it feels to use, not just whether it runs. Most recently, I led an 8-person team through a full software development cycle and served as the Design Lead on HoloOrbits — an educational game from Professor Jina Kang's team at UIUC that teaches the fundamentals of space physics through collaborative play. In both roles, the compass was the same: start from the person on the other side of the screen and work backwards.

Outside

Outside the screen, I have a profound love for writing, music, and pretty much anything creative. I've always thought that this background makes me a better builder — I'm crazy about fine details and consistency, I care deeply about feel, and I ask questions that don't always show up in a product brief.

Next

I'm looking for opportunities where I can bring both sides to the table — the technical foundation and the human instinct. If that sounds like something you're building toward, I'd love to connect!
