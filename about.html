More on Me

I'm Tim, a CS + Education student at UIUC finishing up my final year. I got into computer science because I wanted to build things, and I stayed because I realized the most interesting problem in tech isn't the code, but the people that are using it.

I've always been drawn to the space where technology meets people. That curiosity led me to project management, UX thinking, and building things that don't just work, but feel right to use. Most recently, I led an 8-person team through a full software development cycle and served as a Design Lead on HoloOrbits, a collaborative educational game developed by Professor Jina Kang and her team at UIUC, where I got to think about design, learning, and what it means to build something genuinely useful for students.

Outside the screen, I have profound love for drawing, music, and pretty much anything creative. I think that background makes me a better builder — I notice details, I care about feel, and I ask questions that don't always show up in a product brief.

I'm currently looking for opportunities where I can bring both sides of that to the table — the technical foundation and the human instinct. If that sounds like something you're building toward, I'd love to connect!
